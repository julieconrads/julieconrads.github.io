New preprint on bioRxiv: **[Sequential invasion under disturbance drives functional homogenisation of lake benthos](https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.64898/2026.10.22.760877v1)**. Based on more than 91,000 benthic invertebrates from Lake Zurich, we show that invasive species dominate the shallow lake bottom and that native functional diversity has contracted. The paper also reports the first record of the Quagga mussel in Lake Zurich.
